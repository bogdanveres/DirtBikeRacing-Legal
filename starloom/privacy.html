The short version. The game has no account and no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email address or phone number. Your progress is stored on your device and in your device's own backup. The game shows ads, sells one in-app purchase, the full game, and, where you allow it, counts gameplay events. Two random identifiers leave your device whatever you answer on the consent form, including before you answer and after you refuse: a Firebase installation ID when the game fetches its settings, and a RevenueCat ID when it checks what you own. Section 3 says exactly what happens and when.

Bogdan Veres, Romania, is the data controller for what this policy describes. Write to bogdan.veres@pedalcare.io about anything in it.

3. Before you answer, and if you refuse

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form opens on the first launch. Here is what happens, stated plainly.

  • Before you answer. The check that decides whether a form applies, the Firebase installation ID with the settings fetch, and the RevenueCat ID with the check of what you own have already gone by the time the form is on screen. No gameplay event and no ad request has been sent.
  • If you refuse. Analytics stays off and the events waiting in memory are thrown away. The app instance ID is removed from RevenueCat, so purchases are no longer reported to Google Analytics. Apple's tracking prompt is not shown, because you are not being tracked. The game keeps working in full.
  • What continues after a refusal. The Firebase installation ID goes with each settings fetch and the RevenueCat ID, and on iPhone and iPad the identifier for vendor, with each check of what you own. They are not used for advertising. Ads can still be requested: Google's software reads your refusal itself and limits what it does.
  • One answer decides analytics. The form records your choices purpose by purpose. For analytics and for the link to RevenueCat the game reads one of them, Purpose 1, “store and/or access information on a device”. If you allow that purpose, and Google's platform allows ads on your answer, analytics turns on, whatever you chose for the other purposes. If you refuse it, analytics stays off. The game does not read the measurement purposes separately.
  • If the form cannot load. Analytics and ads stay off and the game asks again later. A form that could not load is never treated as a yes.
  • Where no form applies. Analytics is on from the first launch and ads may be personalised, subject to Apple's prompt on iPhone and iPad and your device's own ad settings.

Apple's tracking prompt

On iPhone and iPad, Apple's prompt asks whether the game may track you across other companies' apps and websites. It appears after Google's form if you allowed it, or at launch where no form applies. If you refuse the form it does not appear, and if you later allow the form through Privacy choices it appears then. If you decline it, ads are not personalised and the game plays the same.

Changing your mind

  • Consent form: in the game's Settings, under Privacy choices. The row is there where a form applies. Your new answer takes effect straight away.
  • Apple's prompt: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.
  • Advertising identifier on Android: Settings → Google → Ads on your device.

Notifications

The game never asks about notifications when it starts. After you first finish Level 1 it asks once, on its hub, whether you want a note when there is news, an update or an offer, and a reminder when the crew is waiting. Only a yes goes on to your phone's own permission prompt, and “Not now” is never asked again.

  • If you say yes and your phone allows it. Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging creates a random registration token for this install of the game, tied on iPhone and iPad to the token Apple's push service gives it, and the game subscribes it to a “news” topic so that we can send you those notes. The token is used only to deliver them, and Google keeps delivery statistics, not linked to you, for the reports we see. Your consent is the basis. The reminder is set on your phone, three days after you leave the game, and cancelled when you come back; nothing is sent for it, and it stops once you have reached the end of the story.
  • Before a yes, after “Not now” and with notifications off. No token exists and nothing is sent for notifications. The game keeps only your answer, on your device.
  • Turning them off. The row Notifications in the game's Settings turns them off at any time: the game leaves the topic, the token is deleted and no reminder is set. Your phone's settings can turn them off too.

4. Your saved game

Your progress, settings, Scrap, what you have bought and the RevenueCat ID that bought it are written to your device. On Android that save, and nothing else from the game, goes into your own Google backup and to a new phone when you transfer. On iPhone and iPad it is part of your device's own backup. Those copies are under your control and we cannot read them.

5. Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google, and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm a purchase and restore it. A refund is yours to ask for from the store you paid.

6. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game, and in its backup until you remove that backup.
  • Gameplay events in Google Analytics: for the period set in Google Analytics, at most 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Purchase records in RevenueCat: for as long as the game needs them to restore what you bought, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • The Firebase installation ID: under Firebase's own retention terms. Deleting the game means a new ID at the next install.
  • Ad data: under Google's own policy.

7. What we do not do

  • We have no account system and do not collect your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • We do not sell your information for money. Personalised advertising through Google can count as “sharing” under some US state laws. Where Google offers a choice about it for your state, it appears in the same Privacy choices row.
  • We do not make automated decisions that have a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

8. Children

The game is for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If your country sets a higher age for consenting to online services by yourself (in Romania, 16), a parent or guardian should give or refuse consent on the form with you. Ad requests carry a maximum content rating of PG. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights and deleting your data

You may ask what we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its use, receive a copy, and withdraw consent at any time, which does not affect what happened before. Write to bogdan.veres@pedalcare.io. We answer within one month.

  • To delete purchase records, include the order number from your store receipt. Nothing else connects a purchase record to you, and the game does not show you its random IDs.
  • Gameplay events are tied only to a random ID that the game does not show you, so we cannot find yours from anything you can tell us. They are deleted when the period in section 6 ends.
  • Your save is removed by deleting the game and its backup. We hold no copy.

You may also complain to a data protection authority. In Romania that is the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP).

10. Information outside your country

Google, Apple and RevenueCat operate internationally and may process information in other countries, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes are announced in the game before they take effect.

12. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: bogdan.veres@pedalcare.io
