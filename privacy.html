Privacy Policy

Dirt Bike Racing: Motocross Game · Last updated 29 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Dirt Bike Racing: Motocross Game (“the game”), published by Bogdan Veres (“we”).

The short version. There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number. Your progress is stored on your own device and in your own iCloud account. The game shows ads and sells in-app purchases, and those two things involve third parties described below.

1. Information the game handles

WhatWhyWho sees it
Game progress — races completed, times, coins, garage, purchases owned So you can keep playing where you left off, and not lose progress if you reinstall Stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account. We have no access to it
Purchase records — which in-app product was bought, and when To deliver what you paid for and to restore it after a reinstall RevenueCat and Apple. Identified by a randomly generated ID, not by your name
Advertising identifier, IP address, ad interactions, coarse device and app information To show ads, limit repeats, measure whether an ad worked, and prevent fraud Google AdMob
Gameplay events — which screens were opened, when a race ended To understand where the game is too hard or unclear Nobody. This stays on your device and is not transmitted

2. What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account or provide any contact details.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We never see your payment card details. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google.
  • We have no access to the copy of your save that is kept in your iCloud account.

3. Your saved game

Your progress is written to your device. On iOS a copy is also written to your own iCloud account using Apple’s key-value storage, so that deleting and reinstalling the game does not lose your campaign, your coins, or a purchase you made. On Android the equivalent copy is made by the system’s own backup service.

That copy lives in your Apple or Google account, under your control. We cannot read it, and it is removed when you remove it from your account or delete the game and its backups.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to confirm that a purchase completed and to restore purchases on a new installation.

RevenueCat identifies your purchases with a randomly generated identifier. It is not linked to your name or email address, and we do not connect it to any other information.

5. Advertising

The game shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Two kinds appear: short full-screen ads between races, and optional ads you choose to watch in exchange for in-game coins. Watching the optional kind is always your decision.

To do this, Google receives your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, and information about how you interacted with an ad. Google’s use of this information is governed by its own privacy policy.

Personalised ads and your choice

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any personalised advertising, using Google’s consent form. You may refuse, in which case you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your answer at any time from the game’s Options screen.

On iOS, Apple additionally asks whether the game may track you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline, advertising remains non-personalised. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

Buying ads out

The game sells a one-off purchase that stops the full-screen ads. The optional coin-paying ads remain available, because they pay you and removing them would be a downgrade.

6. Children

The game is a general-audience racing game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Because the game has no account, we hold almost nothing that identifies you. Even so:

  • Access and deletion. You may ask what we hold and ask us to delete it. Deleting the game and its backup from your Apple or Google account removes your save.
  • Consent. Where consent was the basis for personalised ads, you may withdraw it at any time from the Options screen.
  • Do not sell or share. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California law, and there is nothing to opt out of.

To exercise any of these, write to the address at the bottom of this page. We answer within 30 days.

8. Data outside your country

Our service providers — Apple, Google, and RevenueCat — operate internationally and may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Material changes will be announced in the game before they take effect.
